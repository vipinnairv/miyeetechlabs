About MiyeeFit Pro

Your fitness, weight and veg nutrition in one place

MiyeeFit Pro is a personal fitness, weight reduction and pure vegetarian nutrition tracker from Miyee Tech Labs. It runs in any browser on your phone, tablet or computer, so there is nothing to install. Sign up free with an email and password, and your data is saved to the cloud and syncs across your devices automatically. Prefer not to create an account? You can use the app without one, and your data stays on that device.

Each day, log your food, water, steps and exercise, then see your progress on a clear dashboard with charts for weight trend, calories in versus out, and macros. A daily Fitness Score from 0 to 100 shows how you are doing and tells you exactly what to fix. The built-in Veg Diet Planner offers five meal plans, including Jain options, scaled to your calorie target.

Everything is strictly 100% vegetarian, with no meat, fish, poultry or eggs anywhere. Members can also choose a personal trainer who sets targets and weekly exercises and stays in touch inside the app.

Key features

Everything you need to build healthy habits

Fitness Dashboard

Steps, distance, water, calories burnt, calorie balance and weight progress, with a projected goal date.

Progress charts

Follow your weight trend, calories in versus out, and macros at a glance.

Daily Tracker

A 68-item pure vegetarian Indian and global food database, including roti, dal, paneer, poha, idli, sprouts and curd, plus your own custom foods.

Exercise logging

12 activities, including brisk walking, running, cycling, yoga and Surya Namaskar, swimming and strength training.

Morning check-in

Note your weight, waist, sleep and energy each day.

Daily Fitness Score

A 0 to 100 score built from 7 checks, with clear "what to fix" advice. Food logging is optional, and you still get a fair score without it.

Veg Diet Planner

Five meal plans (High-Protein Indian, Low-GI, Working Professional, Jain and Jain High-Protein) scaled to your calorie target, with meal swaps, one-tap add to your log, and a protein and fibre cheat sheet.

Jain diet option

Suggestions and plans avoid onion, garlic and root vegetables.

Next-meal suggestions

Realistic ideas based on the time of day and the calories and protein you have left.

Works your way

Offline use that syncs when you are back online, light and dark themes, JSON backups, a CSV progress report and an interactive guided tour.

Who it's for

For members, trainers and coaches

Members

Anyone who wants a simple way to track fitness, weight and pure vegetarian nutrition. Choose a listed trainer if you want guidance, tick off the weekly exercises they assign, and reply to their messages. Any target your trainer leaves blank, you set yourself.

Personal trainers and coaches

Your own console to see each client's progress, set daily targets (steps, exercise, water, calories and protein), assign weekly exercises, write instructions and message your clients.

Built on trusted standards

  • Energy and activity: Mifflin-St Jeor BMR, the Compendium of Physical Activities 2024 (net METs) and the ACSM walking equation.
  • Activity and sleep: WHO 2020 physical activity guidelines (150 to 300 minutes a week) and AASM sleep guidance (7 to 9 hours).
  • Hydration: water intake guidance from EFSA and IOM.
  • Body measures: WHO and Asian-Indian BMI categories, NICE waist-to-height ratio and IDF South Asian waist cut-offs.

Privacy & data

  • Passwords are securely hashed, and each account has its own private data vault.
  • Trainers can only see members who have chosen them. You can remove your trainer, reset all your data or delete your account at any time.
  • Use it with an account for cloud sync, or without one, in which case your data stays on that device. The app is hosted on Cloudflare.
